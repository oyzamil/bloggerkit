Archive

Blogger's feed API has no dedicated archive endpoint — archive.* is built on publishedMin/publishedMax range queries, with years() derived from the oldest/newest post.

blog.archive.years()

blog.archive.year(year)

blog.archive.month(year, month)

publishedMin/Max = 1st – 1st next month
