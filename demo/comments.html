Comments

List blog-wide or per-post, and look up a single comment by id.

blog.comments(options)

GET ./comments/default

blog.comments(postId)

GET ./{postId}/comments/default

blog.comment(commentId, postId?)

direct GET with postId, else a paged scan
Passing postId makes one direct request. Without it, blogr scans the blog-wide comments feed in pages of 100 (up to 500) since Blogger has no id-only comment lookup.
